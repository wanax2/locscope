      Getting your location data
      Google moved Timeline onto your phone in 2024–2025, so get both of these. Add everything you have; LocScope removes overlaps.
      
    
      
      
      From your phone (your Timeline now)
      
        Android
      1. Settings → Location → Location services → Timeline
      2. Tap Export Timeline data
      3. Copy Timeline.json to this computer (USB, Drive or email)
      
        iPhone
      1. Google Maps → your picture → Your Timeline
      2. ⋯ → Location & privacy settings → Export Timeline data
      3. Copy location-history.json to this computer
      
      
      Google Takeout (older history)
      
        
      1. Open takeout.google.com
      2. Click Deselect all, then tick Location History (Timeline), and Maps (your places) for saved and labeled places
      3. Next step → .zip, largest size → Create export
      4. Download the zip from Google's email. No need to unzip it.
      
        
      
    
      
    
      Also works
      GPX, TCX, KML, KMZ, GeoJSON, CSV and NMEA files from other apps and GPS units, Garmin and Strava exports, and folders of photos (their GPS tags).
