Very little location history was found. This report is built mostly from saved places, pins and photos. Google now keeps your Timeline on your phone, so Takeout often has none. Export it from the phone and run LocScope again with that file added:
Android: Settings → Location → Location services → Timeline → Export Timeline data (Timeline.json)
iPhone: Google Maps → your picture → Your Timeline → ⋯ → Location & privacy settings → Export Timeline data

Fix-ups

Correct mistakes in Google's data. In any day's timeline (on the map or in Browse), click ✎ next to a stop to rename the place, remove a wrong stop, or ignore a whole day. Your changes are listed here. Click Download fix-ups, then Import on the window's Fix-ups tab, and every future report will use them.
